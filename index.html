🍨 How to Play

Place items: Tap the current item (bobbling above the tray) onto any empty cell. Or tap any item in the tray to place it directly.

Merge: Tap an item on the board to select it (gold glow). Tap another item of the same tier to merge them into a higher tier!

Fulfill orders: When you have an item matching a To-Go Order, select it, then tap the order card (it pulses red). Earn coins! 🪙

Goal: Merge your way to the legendary ✨ Rainbow Sundae and earn as many coins as possible!
